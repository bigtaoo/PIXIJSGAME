# SumQuest

**Tagline:** Tap two numbers that add up to the target. Clear the board before time runs out.

---

## A pure, language-free number puzzle

SumQuest is a fast, minimal matching puzzle built around a single idea: find two numbers on the board that **sum to the target**, tap them, and watch them vanish. No tutorials, no walls of text — the rules reveal themselves in seconds, in any language.

## How to play

- Each level shows a **target number**.
- Tap any **two tiles** whose values add up to that target to clear them.
- Clear every tile to beat the level.
- Beat the clock: each target gives you a fresh **30 seconds**.

## Features

- **19 hand-tuned levels** — the target climbs from 6 all the way to 99, so the challenge ramps up naturally.
- **3 lives, one comeback** — run out of time and you lose a life; out of lives, watch a short ad to revive once.
- **Star rating** — finish with time to spare to earn up to **3 stars**.
- **Daily Challenge** — everyone plays the same board each day (fixed seed), no lives, pure score chase.
- **Plays anywhere** — runs right in your browser, desktop or mobile, portrait or landscape.
- **Zero text, zero clutter** — clean, focused visuals that get out of your way.

## Controls

Mouse click or touch — that's it.

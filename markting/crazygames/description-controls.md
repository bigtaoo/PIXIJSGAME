## Description

SumQuest is a fast-paced number puzzle where quick math meets quick reflexes. The board fills with scattered numbers and shows you a target value — tap any two numbers that add up to that target to clear them from the grid. Wipe the whole board before the clock runs out to advance.

Race through 19 hand-tuned levels with target numbers climbing from 6 all the way to 99. Each level throws five targets at you, and every new target tops up your clock by 30 seconds — so keep the matches flowing to stay alive. You start with three lives; let the timer hit zero and you lose one. Down to your last life? Watch a short ad to jump straight back in.

Finish a level with time to spare and you'll earn up to three stars, so there's always a reason to come back and beat your best run.

Feeling competitive? Dive into the Daily Challenge — everyone plays the exact same board, generated from a shared daily seed, with no lives to slow you down. Just chase the highest score and climb the leaderboard.

With clean visuals, instant pick-up-and-play controls, and difficulty that scales naturally as you sharpen up, SumQuest is the perfect brain-teaser for a quick break or a long high-score grind.

### Features

- 19 levels of escalating number-matching puzzles (targets from 6 to 99)
- Beat-the-clock gameplay — every target adds 30 seconds
- 1–3 star ratings to chase perfection on every level
- Daily Challenge with a shared seed and a global leaderboard
- One-tap controls — easy to learn, tough to master

## Controls

Tap (or click) two numbers whose sum equals the target value to clear them from the board. Clear every number before the timer reaches zero to complete the level.

- **Tap / Left-click** — select a number
- **Tap a second number** — clear the pair (their sum must equal the target)
- **Pause button** (top of the screen) — pause or resume the game
